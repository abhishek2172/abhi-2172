## Industry Context

The image consulting industry has grown significantly since its emergence in the late 1970s and early 1980s. Originally serving politicians, models, and celebrities, it has expanded to serve professionals seeking competitive advantages in their careers. The demand for image consultants has risen due to:

- Increased competition in professional markets
- Growing awareness of personal branding importance
- Expansion beyond traditional "makeovers" to include behavior, communication, and presentation skills
- Rise of business casual dress codes requiring professional guidance
